## The gate

`plan/backlog/_meta.yaml`, milestone P1:

> qc-vm matches id's VM statement by statement on the id 1.01 and rerelease corpus; parser round-trips the corpus losslessly

The gate issue's acceptance restates it as "zero VM divergences on id 1.01 (id qcc) and rerelease (pinned FTEQCC) with at least 90% statement coverage; lossless parse of the whole corpus", subject to [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-differential-coverage-bar.md), which sets the VM oracle's hard bar to zero divergences and does not gate the flat 90% statement figure (quoted below).
